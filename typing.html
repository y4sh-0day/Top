How to place your hands (Mac)How to type fast on a phone
  1. Find the two little bumps on the F and J keys. Put your left index finger on F and your right index finger on J. You can find them without looking.
  2. Rest the other fingers next to them: left hand on A S D F, right hand on J K L ;. This is the home row.
  3. Rest both thumbs lightly on the space bar. The ⌘ command keys next to it are also pressed with your thumbs.
  4. Each finger owns one coloured column on the keyboard below. Reach up or down, press, then go back to the home row.
  5. For capital letters, hold shift with the pinky of the other hand. On a Mac, return is Enter and delete is Backspace (both right pinky).
  6. Keep your wrists slightly raised, sit up straight, and look at the screen, not your hands.
  7. Accuracy first. Aim for 95% or more, and the speed comes by itself. Practise 10 minutes a day.
